# HARMONY IA LÍA — Public Physical Embodiment Proposal 2032–2035

**The pieces already exist. The challenge is integration.**

HARMONY IA LÍA publishes a deliberately limited technical hypothesis for a human-compatible humanoid body: lightweight load-bearing structure, compliant precision actuation, continuous proprioception, multimodal electronic skin, dexterous tactile hands, expressive HRI, maintainable synthetic surface layers, and a strict safety boundary between high-level AI cognition and deterministic physical control.

This repository is **not** the project's private software repository. It contains no cognitive implementation, private memory, prompts, datasets, credentials, internal architecture, source code, private roadmap, or unpublished embodiment know-how.

## Five-layer sensorimotor envelope

1. **Internal structure** — Ti-6Al-4V at critical nodes; aluminium/CFRP where reduced distal inertia is valuable.
2. **Artificial muscle / actuation layer** — PMSM/BLDC, precision transmissions, and compliance where appropriate.
3. **Distributed sensory network** — joint encoders, IMU, torque/current estimation, force/tactile/slip/strain/temperature sensing.
4. **Synthetic dermis** — elastomeric/load-distributing protective layers around sensors and structure.
5. **Synthetic epidermis** — serviceable silicone/TPE-style outer contact layer for friction, environmental protection and socially legible appearance.

Electronic skin supplies exteroceptive contact information; deep proprioception still depends on joint, inertial and force state. A useful body schema should fuse both.

## Convergence references

Publicly documented capabilities from OpenAI, Tesla Optimus, Unitree G1, ENGINEAI and Engineered Arts/Ameca are cited as **independent technical references only**. No partnership, sponsorship or endorsement is claimed.

A high-level AI layer may be suitable for language, tool use, task reasoning and intent generation. Critical balance, collision avoidance, torque, force limits, emergency stop and low-level actuation should remain under independent deterministic safety control.

## Compute, memory and storage envelope

A human-compatible humanoid also needs a computational nervous system. The public design baseline proposes **64 GB as a practical minimum, 128 GB recommended, and 128–256 GB+ for future-ready local multimodal workloads**. For local storage, use **2 TB NVMe minimum, 4 TB recommended, and 4–8 TB+ for development/research**. Safety-critical motor control should remain on independent deterministic controllers.

A 2026 reference point is NVIDIA Jetson AGX Thor, which exposes 128 GB LPDDR5X in its T5000 configuration; this is a contemporary benchmark, not a fixed 2032–2035 hardware choice. See `docs/10_COMPUTE_MEMORY_STORAGE.md`.
