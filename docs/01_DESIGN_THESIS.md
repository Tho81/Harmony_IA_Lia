# 01 · Design thesis — the pieces already exist

The working hypothesis of HARMONY IA LÍA is that a more capable social humanoid does not require every subsystem to be invented by one organization. The missing step may be **system-level integration** across technologies that have matured in different parts of the ecosystem.

## Design target

A human-compatible body should optimize a multi-objective envelope rather than a single headline metric:

- specific strength and fatigue life;
- low distal inertia;
- adequate torque density;
- controllable compliance;
- tactile spatial resolution and bandwidth;
- continuous joint/inertial state estimation;
- maintainability and replaceable surface modules;
- human-readable motion and facial signaling;
- safe failure modes and independent physical supervision.

## Architecture principle

```text
load-bearing skeleton
        +
compliant actuation
        +
deep proprioception
        +
distributed tactile envelope
        +
synthetic dermis / epidermis
        +
expressive HRI
        +
safety-gated cognitive interface
```

No single layer substitutes for the others. A realistic skin without proprioception is not sufficient; high torque without compliance is not sufficient; expressive behavior without safe physical control is not sufficient.

## 2032–2035 horizon

The date range is a **design horizon**, not a prediction that a specific product will exist on a fixed date. It is used to structure a question: what combination of already-visible technical trajectories would make a convincing, safe and serviceable embodied assistant plausible during that period?
