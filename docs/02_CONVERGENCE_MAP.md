## Strategic interpretation

The convergence thesis is intentionally modular:

```text
high-level cognition / tools
            ↓
structured intent interface
            ↓
physical safety supervisor
            ↓
whole-body planning
            ↓
local deterministic controllers
            ↓
actuators + sensors
```

This separation allows the cognitive layer to evolve without giving a generative model direct authority over torque, balance or emergency-stop behavior.
