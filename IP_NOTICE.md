# Public disclosure and intellectual-property boundary

This repository is a **sanitized public research artifact**, not a release of the full HARMONY IA LÍA project.

## Purpose of publication

The public objective is to make a technically credible physical-embodiment hypothesis discoverable to researchers and developers working on humanoid robotics, materials, tactile sensing, compliant actuation and HRI.

## Reserved material

No rights or access are granted to unpublished implementation details, private cognitive software, memory systems, model-routing logic, internal experiments, source repositories, local infrastructure, private datasets, voice assets, private visual references or proprietary know-how.

## Patent / novelty caution

Public disclosure can affect patent novelty. Under Article 54 EPC, material made available to the public before filing can become prior art. Accordingly, this repository intentionally stays at the level of **public-safe concepts, standard equations, externally documented technology references and high-level integration hypotheses**. Any future implementation detail that may merit patent/design protection should be reviewed before publication.

EPO reference: https://www.epo.org/en/legal/epc/2020/a54.html

Nothing here should be read as a representation that a disclosed concept is patentable, unpatentable, exclusively owned, or free of third-party rights.
